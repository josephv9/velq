SHOW THE WORK

Simple, checkable arithmetic.

The label calculator uses values entered by the reader. It does not supply a recommendation or infer a missing label value.

Read our editorial policy ↗
Cost per complete serving
Package price ÷ labeled servings per package
Cost per 10 g protein
Cost per serving × 10 ÷ protein grams per serving
Cost per 5 g fiber
Cost per serving × 5 ÷ fiber grams per serving

Price must be nonnegative and serving count greater than zero. Invalid or missing required inputs prompt a correction. Blank optional amounts show “Not entered”; zero protein or fiber produces an “N/A” nutrient-cost result. USD results round to two decimal places; positive amounts under one cent display “<$0.01”.

These calculations do not establish ingredient quality, safety, clinical equivalence or suitability. Read each product’s current full label and directions.
