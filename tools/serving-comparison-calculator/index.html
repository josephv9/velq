See the serving calculation

A full labeled serving can contain more than one gummy. The example below shows why comparing a serving with a single piece changes the result.

How the serving math works

Per piece = labeled amount ÷ pieces per serving
Your portion = per-piece amount × pieces in your portion
Per 100 kcal = labeled amount ÷ labeled calories × 100

Keep every number tied to the same exact product and labeled serving. A pouch, an adult serving, and a child serving can describe different amounts.

A worked example

Fictional gummy A lists 5 g fiber in a two-piece serving. Its calculated fiber per piece is 5 ÷ 2 = 2.5 g. One piece represents half of that labeled serving.

Fictional gummy B lists 6 g fiber in a three-piece serving. Its calculated fiber per piece is 6 ÷ 3 = 2 g. B has more fiber per full serving, while A has more per piece in this example.

What a blank label field means

A missing protein figure is not the same as a label reporting 0 g protein. A bound such as “less than 1 g” is not an exact 1 g. Presets leave such values blank instead of turning them into an unsupported number.
