What changes when you compare one gummy?

Metamucil and OLLY both report 5 g of dietary fiber in the serving used here. Metamucil uses three gummies; OLLY uses two. Dividing by the piece count gives approximately 1.67 g versus 2.5 g per gummy. The advertised fiber amount can match even when the number of pieces differs. See manufacturer sources below.

Vitafusion Fiber Well publishes a panel with two serving columns: 5 g fiber for two gummies and 7.5 g for three. Both normalize to 2.5 g per gummy. We use the three-gummy column highlighted on the product page. The two columns alone do not establish a formula change.

Why the product version matters

The OAKA source in this snapshot is the current Mixed Berry pouch product. It establishes nutrients per pouch, but the inspected page and label do not establish an exact gummy count. We therefore leave its per-gummy figures blank. Combining that pouch with the eight-gummy serving of an older product listing would create a serving that the sources do not support.

To compare your own package, open the serving comparison calculator. Use the serving and nutrient numbers from the same label. The FDA explains that a Nutrition Facts serving size is not an instruction for how much to eat; supplement directions also need to be followed.

Methodology you can reproduce

Research question
How do the published full-serving and per-piece nutrient amounts differ across selected protein and fiber gummies?
Sample selection
Purposive examples related to Velq’s existing protein and fiber gummy comparison pages: four fiber-gummy and three protein-gummy products with accessible manufacturer information. This is not a comprehensive market census or a representative sample.
Observation
Manufacturer product pages and linked label images inspected on 20 October 2026 (UTC). Each row identifies the exact variant and the primary source. A website statement and a readable label panel are recorded as different evidence types.
Transcription
Copy only established serving values from a matching product version. A blank field becomes JSON null and an empty CSV cell. Less-than statements are stored as qualifiers with no invented exact number. A zero is entered only when the source reports zero.
Normalization
Per gummy = labeled grams ÷ gummies in that labeled serving. Only rows with both an exact numerator and a verified nonzero piece count enter per-gummy comparisons. We do not borrow counts, calories or grams from another SKU.
Summary denominator
The serving-size and dietary-fiber range above use only the four fiber-gummy rows. The three protein-gummy rows are shown separately. Different source ingredients are not treated as clinically interchangeable.
Exclusions
Velq’s development formula, unverified prices, consumer reviews and clinical outcomes are excluded. PROTEDY was considered but its live source could not be reliably retrieved during this observation, so cached search figures were not added to the primary dataset. OAKA’s piece count is excluded from normalization.
Limitations
Manufacturer claims have not been independently verified against a purchased package or laboratory analysis. Websites and labels can contain inconsistencies or change after this snapshot. A higher number is not a general product recommendation. Sampling and commercial affiliation limit how these findings can be interpreted.
Reproduction
Download the dated JSON or CSV. Divide each available fiber or protein amount by its matching piece count, leaving unavailable or bounded values uncalculated. The study table and chart are generated from the same JSON snapshot.

Questions about these findings

Does “per gummy” tell me how many gummies to take?

No. It is a mathematical restatement of the published serving. Follow the product directions and do not use normalized amounts to work out a supplement dose.

Why does this study leave some numbers blank?

A missing nutrient or serving count is not evidence of zero. A number is calculated only when the exact inputs are established by the source for the same product version.

Is this independent clinical or laboratory research?

No. This is Velq’s original compilation and descriptive analysis of manufacturer-published information. Velq has a commercial interest in this category. No effectiveness, absorption, tolerance or ingredient-purity findings are established.

Why are Velq’s proposed grams not in the table?

Velq is still in development. An unfinished target formula cannot be compared as if it were a verified commercial label.
