Made for people who hate heavy shakes

Most protein powders were designed to be milkshakes. If you've ever poured one down the sink after two sips, you know the problem: thick texture, dairy heaviness, and a chore where a drink should be. Velq takes the opposite approach, protein at a meaningful dose, delivered like a protein water, in a lemonade you'd choose to drink anyway.

It also closes two more gaps in the same glass: most adults fall short on daily fiber, and plain water alone doesn't replace the electrolytes a full day takes out. One stick covers all three routines, more on how that works in our guide to mixing protein and electrolytes together.

How to drink it

  1. Tear: open one single-serve stick.
  2. Pour: empty it into 16 oz of cold water.
  3. Shake: until fully mixed. Add ice and enjoy.
