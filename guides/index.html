How to evaluate protein and fiber gummies

Compare the complete serving listed on each nutrition label rather than relying on the largest number displayed on a package. Gummy products can differ in ingredients, pieces per serving and nutrient amounts.

  1. Look at the full serving. Count the gummies or packets required, then compare grams of protein and fiber per complete serving.
  2. Identify the ingredients. Check the protein source, fiber ingredient, allergens and complete ingredient list.
  3. Read the other nutrition details. Compare sugar and sweeteners, and check for declared caffeine or other active ingredients.
  4. Evaluate value. Use cost per complete serving, not only the sticker price of a box or pouch.
  5. Confirm product readiness. Velq is in development. Final product facts should be checked against the finished label once available.
