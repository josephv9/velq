CALCULATOR METHODS
Cost per complete serving = package price / labeled servings per package.
Cost per 10 g protein = cost per serving * 10 / protein grams per serving.
Cost per 5 g fiber = cost per serving * 5 / fiber grams per serving.
Price must be nonnegative and serving count greater than zero.
Invalid or missing required inputs prompt a correction. Blank optional amounts
show "Not entered"; zero protein or fiber produces an N/A nutrient-cost result.
USD results round to two decimal places; positive amounts under one cent
display "<$0.01".
These calculations do not establish ingredient quality, safety, clinical
equivalence or suitability. Read current full product labels and directions.
