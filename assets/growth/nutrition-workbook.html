Choose a shelf-stable snack combination

Filter by the ingredients in these ideas, not certified allergen status. Always check packaging and cross-contact warnings. No refrigeration refers to unopened items explicitly labeled shelf-stable.

  • Dry-roasted edamame with whole-grain crackers: check soy, wheat, and storage directions.
  • Nut-butter packet with whole fruit: keep fruit whole and check nut allergens.
  • Shelf-stable salmon pouch with whole-grain crispbread: eat promptly after opening; check fish and wheat allergens.
  • Dry-roasted chickpeas with pumpkin seeds: check processing, added ingredients, and cross-contact warnings.

FDA food-storage guidance. Follow each manufacturer’s directions.
