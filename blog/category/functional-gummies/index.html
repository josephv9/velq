THE BASICS

What makes a gummy functional?

Functional gummies are generally marketed around added ingredients or an intended use beyond taste. That description alone does not establish an effect or nutritional value. Products may be sold as conventional foods or dietary supplements, and the applicable label matters.

01

Identify the ingredient

A protein gummy, a fiber gummy and a caffeinated gummy serve different purposes. Look for the actual ingredient and its amount per complete serving.

02

Compare realistic portions

Two gummies in one product may not equal two gummies in another. Compare the full labeled serving, ingredient type and how many servings you would need.

03

Understand the limits

A convenient format does not replace a varied diet or guarantee a benefit. Check warnings, allergens, caffeine content and directions, especially when combining products.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

What are functional gummies?

They are gummies marketed for a nutritional or other intended function, often with added ingredients such as protein, fiber or caffeine. The actual formulation, serving size and evidence matter more than the category name.

Are functional gummies the same as vitamins?

No. Some gummies contain vitamins; others contain macronutrients, caffeine or other ingredients. Check whether the product uses a Nutrition Facts or Supplement Facts label and follow its directions.

Can I combine multiple functional gummies?

Check whether ingredients overlap, especially caffeine or supplemental ingredients. Follow the labeled directions and consult a qualified clinician if you take medication or have relevant health concerns.
