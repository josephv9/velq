Shipping

Where we ship

All 50 US states. International shipping isn't available for the founding batch, join the email list and we'll notify you when it opens.

Tracking

You'll receive a tracking link by email the day your box ships. Founding orders ship in the order they were placed.

Returns and refunds

Unopened boxes

Return any unopened box within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, no questions asked. Email us and we'll send instructions.

Opened boxes

If you've tried it and it isn't for you, contact us within 30 days. We'll refund your first box even if it's opened, once per customer.

If something arrives damaged

Send us a photo within 14 days and we'll replace the box at no cost. No need to return the damaged one.

How refunds arrive

Refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days after we process them.
