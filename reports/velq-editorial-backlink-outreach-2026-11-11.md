The original label audit covers **seven selected manufacturer-published gummy product versions** (four fiber, three protein). It compares complete labeled servings with per-piece arithmetic where a reliable piece count was available. This is a dated, nonrandom, descriptive label study—not laboratory testing, clinical research, a representative census, a health-outcomes ranking, or independent product verification. Velq's own development targets are excluded from the research data.

## Outreach targets
Prioritize relevant editorial writers, health and nutrition newsletters, independent fitness publications, consumer-shopping comparison writers, and registered-dietitian-authored explainers who publish serving-size or gummy-label education. Pitch to an identified author only when the article is genuinely on topic. Avoid bulk paid-link placements, irrelevant directories, and manufactured testimonials.

## Outreach rules and measurement
1. Verify the exact author, publication, article fit, and preferred contact route. No purchased email lists.
2. Keep claims aligned with the dated sources; check any current price or product availability before stating it.
3. Disclose that Velq is developing its own gummy product. Never imply a clinical or dietitian review.
4. Send one relevant personalized pitch, with at most one respectful follow-up when appropriate.
5. Track dates, publication, contact, topic, response, earned editorial link, and referral sessions; count **only verified links**, not pitches sent.
6. In Google Search Console, monitor organic impressions and clicks to the study page; in GA4, track organic visitors who later complete the server-confirmed waitlist signup.
